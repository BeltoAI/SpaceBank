Privacy Policy

Your data should not need gravity either.

This Privacy Policy explains what SpaceBank may collect through the website, prototype, waitlist, receipt pages, and related demo experiences.

1. Information we collect

Waitlist data

Name, email, company, role, and any message you choose to submit.

Usage data

Pages viewed, device/browser information, referral source, timestamps, and general interaction analytics.

Demo data

Prototype entries, generated receipt details, simulated destinations, and other information created inside the demo.

Communications

Messages you send to us and responses we provide.

2. How we use information

  • To operate the website and prototype.
  • To manage the waitlist and send launch updates.
  • To improve product design, reliability, security, and user experience.
  • To respond to inquiries and prevent misuse.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use waitlist information to make real credit, banking, lending, or investment decisions.

4. Service providers

We may use hosting, analytics, email, form, security, and infrastructure vendors to operate SpaceBank. These providers may process information on our behalf.

5. Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies, local storage, and analytics tools to understand site performance and improve the experience. Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, unless a longer retention period is required for legal, security, or business reasons.

7. Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted by contacting us. You may also unsubscribe from non-essential emails.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service is perfectly secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as SpaceBank evolves. The updated version will be posted here.
