Banking for a multiplanet species.

SpaceBank is the financial layer for a multiplanet civilization — one account that moves with you across Earth, orbit, the Moon, and Mars. We monetize cross-border payments and mission treasury on Earth today, and become the default settlement rail as the space economy comes online.

Problem

Money stops at the atmosphere.

Humanity is going multiplanet, but money still stops at the atmosphere. Today's banking rails assume one planet, instant links, and a branch nearby. Cross-border payments are already slow and costly; cross-planetary value transfer doesn't exist.

Solution

One account that moves between worlds.

Belto's orbital software is the wedge today; SpaceBank is the financial layer it grows into. A latency-aware account and settlement network. Value moves between worlds the only way that scales — as light, over optical laser links between orbital relays — with settlement designed for distance and delay rather than fighting it.

How it works

Value travels as light.

A transfer originates at a SpaceBank relay in Mars orbit, crosses deep space via optical laser to a relay in Earth orbit, then settles into Earth's banking rails — arriving whole, minutes later, not "instantly."
