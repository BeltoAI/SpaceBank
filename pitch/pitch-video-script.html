SpaceBank · Belto Inc.

2-Minute Pitch Video — Script

Target: ~2:00 · ~300 words at a calm pace. Replace every [bracketed] field. Read the italic lines on camera; grey lines are delivery direction, not spoken.

0:00 – 0:15 · Hook

"In the next twenty years, people won't just visit space — they'll live and work there. But there's one thing that still stops at the edge of the atmosphere: your money."

On screen: Earth shot from the site. Calm, confident open. Let "your money" land before the cut.

0:15 – 0:30 · Team intro

"We're [Founder] and [Founder] — the team behind Belto. We build at frontier speed, and we're here to bank the space economy. We call it SpaceBank."

On screen: Team on camera, or names over the SpaceBank logo. Keep it warm and quick — one line each if you split it.

0:30 – 0:55 · Problem

"Today's banking rails assume one planet, instant connections, and a branch down the street. Cross-border payments are already slow and expensive. Cross-planetary? It simply doesn't exist. As launch costs fall and the space economy heads toward one-point-eight trillion dollars by 2035, that gap becomes a real, expensive problem."

On screen: The "how your money crosses the void" section / market stat. Slow down on the numbers.

0:55 – 1:25 · Solution & product

"SpaceBank is one account that moves with you across Earth, orbit, the Moon, and Mars. Value travels the only way that scales across the solar system — as light, over optical laser links between orbital relays, with settlement built for distance instead of fighting it. We built our first prototype this weekend on Gemini and Google AI Studio, hosted on Google Cloud Run — and it's live."

On screen: Quick cut to the prototype / how-it-works animation. Show, don't just tell.

1:25 – 1:50 · Why it matters now

"And we don't have to wait for Mars to earn. The same rails power global accounts, mission treasury for space teams, remote-site payments, and an orbital vault — security that's air-gapped by distance. That's revenue on Earth today, into a market no one owns tomorrow."

On screen: Use-cases section with the shooting stars. Energy lifts here.

1:50 – 2:00 · Close

"SpaceBank — the first bank beyond Earth. Join us at belto.space."

On screen: Logo + belto.space + waitlist URL. End on a clean, confident beat.

Delivery tips: smile on the hook and the close; pause a half-beat after each section; keep energy steady, not rushed. If you run long, trim the second half of the Problem segment first.
